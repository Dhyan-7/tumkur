Key Characteristics

The Four Pillars of Tumkur

A unique confluence of agricultural abundance, deep humanitarian spirituality, educational prowess, and futuristic manufacturing corridors.

🥥

Kalpataru Nadu

Tumkur is globally recognized as the "Land of the Wish-granting Coconut Trees". Thousands of hectares of lush green plantations produce world-renowned coconuts, tender coconut water, and copra trade markets in Tiptur.

Tiptur Copra Hub Coir Industry Lush Groves
🎓

Vidyanagari

Celebrated as an educational sanctuary with top-tier universities, medical colleges, and engineering powerhouses such as SIT (Siddaganga Institute of Technology), Tumkur University, and Sri Siddhartha Academy.

SIT Campus Tumkur University Medical & Tech
🛕

Spiritual Sanctuary

The seat of sacred compassion championed by Dr. Sri Sri Shivakumara Swamiji. Ancient Hoysala architecture at Kaidala, Devarayanadurga hill temples, and historic cave retreats like Siddara Betta offer deep solace.

Siddaganga Mutt Devarayanadurga Jakanachari Legacy
⚡

Smart Industrial Node

As a prime node of the Chennai-Bengaluru-Mumbai Industrial Corridor (CBIC), Tumkur hosts Vasanthanarasapura Industrial Mega Park, the India Food Park, aerospace ventures, and high-speed multi-lane transit.

Sacred Humanitarian Heritage

Sri Siddaganga Mutt

"Kayakave Kailasa — Work is Worship"

Sri Siddaganga Mutt is a revered centuries-old Lingayat monastic institution situated on a rocky hill just 5 km east of Tumkur city. The mutt was sanctified and brought to global prominence by Karnataka's beloved centenarian saint, Padma Bhushan Dr. Sri Sri Sri Shivakumara Swamiji (1907–2019), venerated as the "Walking God" (Nadedaduva Devaru).

🍚

📖

Flavors of Kalpataru Nadu

The Legendary Thatte Idli & Food Trail

No journey to or through Tumkur is complete without savoring its soft, cloud-like plate idlis bathed in fragrant coconut chutney.

Iconic Breakfast Tradition

Tumkur Thatte Idli (ತಟ್ಟೆ ಇಡ್ಲಿ)

Steamed in wide, flat circular clay/metal plates (Thatte in Kannada), these idlis are famously pillowy, porous, and super soft. Served hot with a generous scoop of home-churned butter (Benne), spicy red coconut chutney, and podi.

1
Kalyani & Shivanna Thatte Idli Hubs
Historic eateries along the highway and city centre that have served generations of travelers.
2
Crispy Uddin Vada & Ghee Podi
Golden crisp exterior with an airy soft interior, dipped in freshly ground coconut chutney.
3
Fresh Kalpataru Tender Coconut
Naturally sweet, electrolyte-rich tender coconut water straight from nearby Tiptur farms.
☕ Pair with authentic Tumkur Filter Kaapi for the complete experience!

Getting to Tumkur

Located strategically on National Highway 48 (NH-48), Tumkur serves as the north-western gateway to Bengaluru and the heart of southern India.

🚆

Tumkur Railway Junction (TK)

A major junction on South Western Railway. Vande Bharat, Intercity, and Jan Shatabdi express trains connect daily with Bengaluru, Hubballi, Mumbai, and Belagavi in ~1 hour.

🚌

Express Highway (NH-48) & KSRTC

Direct 6-lane elevated highway from Peenya/Yeshwantpur. Non-stop KSRTC Airavat, EV-Powerplus, and Flybus airport shuttles operate round the clock.

✈️

Kempegowda International Airport

Just ~80 km away. Connected smoothly via Dobbaspet / STRR (Satellite Town Ring Road), bypassing inner Bengaluru city traffic in approximately 85 minutes.
